# RSVP Desktop Reader

A lightweight, fully offline speed-reading app that flashes transcript text
one word at a time, so you can read faster without your eyes scanning back
and forth across a page.

## What it does

RSVP Desktop Reader takes raw video transcripts, strips out the timestamps,
and flashes them word by word in a fixed spot on screen. Each word's Optimal
Recognition Point, the letter your eye naturally lands on to recognize the
word fastest, is highlighted and held in the same position from one word to
the next, so your eye never has to search for where to look.

## The idea

RSVP, Rapid Serial Visual Presentation, shows text one word at a time in a
single fixed location instead of laid out across a page. Normal reading makes
your eyes jump from word to word and often backtrack. RSVP removes that, since
the words come to a stationary point instead of your eyes having to find them.

The Optimal Recognition Point is the specific letter within a word where your
eye focuses to recognize it fastest, usually just left of center and shifting
with the word's length. The app calculates that letter for every word, bolds
it in a distinct color, and keeps it aligned in the exact same screen position
word to word.
